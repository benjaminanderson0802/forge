# Layer 1D: the always-on service

> Plan task P1D. Serves `docs/specs/layer-1-design.md` §5 and the 1D gate in §7 ("kill mid-step; restart after crash; active vs idle behaviour"), implementing D-007, D-018, D-019, D-020 and D-024. Builds on `core/bootstrap.py` (rules R1–R40), `core/agents.py`, `core/usage.py` and `scripts/start_conductor.ps1`. Other agents are building 1C and 1E at the same time, so the `core/bootstrap.py` edits stay small and additive; the service itself lives in the new module `core/service.py`.

## Not in 1D (honest scope)

- **Parallel agents while idle.** The conductor runs one stage, so one agent, at a time. `mode()` computes `max_agents`, and the active-mode limit (one agent) holds by construction, but running up to `max_parallel_idle` agents at once needs a parallel conductor. That is a larger change to `core/bootstrap.py` and should be its own task after 1C and 1E merge.
- **Browsers.** Nothing in Forge launches a browser yet; `mode()["browsers"]` is the flag future browser tasks must obey.
- **The status page and email digest** themselves are 1E. They read `status.json` and call `service.request_stop()` / `service.wake()`.
- **Judge commands** (tests, drills, mutation) aren't interrupted by KILL mid-command; they are bounded by `test_timeout_s`, and nothing is recorded after KILL.

## Tasks

1. **T1D1** `core/service.py`: `idle_seconds()`, `mode()`. Test: `tests/core/test_service_activity.py`.
2. **T1D2** `core/service.py`: `Health` (heartbeat thread, stall exit), `nap()`, `serve()`, `snapshot()`, CLI; `core/bootstrap.py`: `run()` gains an `on_step` hook and returns `"killed"` after a step that ended with KILL set; `main run` uses `serve()`. Test: `tests/core/test_service.py`.
3. **T1D3** `core/agents.py`: `launch(..., should_stop=)`, a live-process registry and `kill_live()`; `core/usage.py`: holds and the runs-per-day cap; `core/bootstrap.py`: KILL before every launch, KILL-only change is a stop, limit windows become holds. Tests: `tests/core/test_service_kill.py`, `tests/core/test_usage_holds.py`.
4. **T1D4** `core/service.py`: `watchdog()`; `scripts/start_conductor.ps1`: the watchdog task. Gate drills: `tests/core/test_service_drills.py`.

Every task is test-first; `python -m unittest discover -s tests/core` and `python drills/run_drills.py` stay green after each.
